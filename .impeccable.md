# Design Context

## Users

**Primary**: SDRs, sales reps, founders, and growth teams at B2B companies who need to research target accounts and generate personalized outreach at scale.

**Context**: They're busy, juggling multiple tools, measured on pipeline generated. They land on this page after hearing about "AI that does 4 hours of research in 30 seconds" — they're skeptical but curious. They want proof it works before committing.

**Job to be done**: "Help me find the right people at target companies and write outreach that actually gets replies — without me doing all the manual grunt work."

**Emotions to evoke**: Confidence (this actually works), relief (I can stop manual research), trust (this isn't vaporware), momentum (I can start today).

---

## Brand Personality

**3 words**: Calm, capable, opinionated

- **Calm**: Not shouty. No false urgency. The product speaks for itself.
- **Capable**: Technical depth shows through. This isn't a thin wrapper — it runs real research pipelines.
- **Opinionated**: We have a point of view on how outbound *should* work (research → pain points → decision makers → outreach). The product encodes that workflow.

---

## Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone**: Calm productivity — think Notion, Linear, early Stripe. Clean typography, generous whitespace, restrained color, purposeful motion. Light mode default (broad audience, daytime use, trust signal).

**Color strategy**: 
- Neutral base tinted toward a deep slate/blue brand hue (not purple, not generic blue)
- Single accent color used sparingly (10% rule) — primary CTAs, key highlights
- No gradients on text. No glowing borders. No "AI sparkle" aesthetics.

**Typography**: 
- Display: A distinctive but readable serif or high-contrast sans (editorial feel, conveys depth)
- Body: A refined, highly legible sans with good numerical tabular figures (for metrics)
- Reject: Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, IBM Plex, Instrument Sans — the AI defaults

**Anti-references**: 
- Generic SaaS hero: huge H1, 3-column feature grid, testimonial carousel, footer with 50 links
- "AI-first" landing pages: purple gradients, sparkle icons, "powered by GPT-4" badges
- Over-animated scroll-jacking experiences

---

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — Demonstrate the pipeline (research → pain points → decision makers → outreach) visually. Let the product flow be the hero.

2. **Earn trust through specificity** — Real screenshots, real output examples, concrete numbers. No "10x your productivity" fluff.

3. **One primary action** — Every page section funnels toward "Start free trial." No competing CTAs.

4. **Respect the user's time** — Scannable. No fluff copy. Fast load. No layout shift.

5. **Technical credibility without jargon** — Show the pipeline stages, mention Lua/ai tools, but explain in plain English what each step produces.
